# Legal review checklist

Repeat these checks for each source and publication. This checklist aids compliance but is not legal advice.

- [ ] Identify exact upstream version, authors, original URL, archive checksum, copyright and applicable **data** license.
- [ ] Keep all original source copyright/attribution information; distinguish a software-code license from the data license.
- [ ] **KOReader:** retain each ZIP's own LICENSE and ATTRIBUTION.
- [ ] **FreeDict:** inspect each TEI header and comply with its specific GPL/GFDL or other terms and source-distribution requirements.
- [ ] **WikDict, Wiktionary / Kaikki:** preserve applicable CC BY-SA text licensing, link to license, attribution, indication of modifications and adapted-data ShareAlike.
- [ ] **Tatoeba:** distinguish CC BY 2.0 France from expressly designated CC0; record author, sentence ID, source URL and translation ID. No audio is included.
- [ ] **Open English WordNet:** preserve CC BY 4.0 **plus Princeton WordNet and Open English WordNet team credits** and original WordNet notices.
- [ ] **Open Multilingual Wordnet:** consult the original license for **each component WordNet**, beyond shorthand in index.toml; retain specific citation and Bond/Foster attribution.
- [ ] **ConceptNet:** use the original [DATA-CREDITS.md](https://github.com/commonsense/conceptnet5/blob/master/DATA-CREDITS.md) for CC BY-SA **data** licensing; the source software LICENSE.txt is not the graph data license.
- [ ] Record every repackaging, normalization, filtered extract or modification.
- [ ] Check mixed-license compatibility before commercial distribution of any single combined adapted database.
- [ ] Publish the correct main, lexical or raw release manifest and matching third-party rights notices.
- [ ] Verify actual published SHA-256 and do not mistake technical integrity for linguistic correctness.
- [ ] Exclude or quarantine unclear/unlicensed source materials and obtain legal review where needed.

Official references: [OEWN license](https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/blob/main/LICENSE.md), [OMW license metadata](https://github.com/omwn/omw-data/blob/main/index.toml), [ConceptNet credits](https://github.com/commonsense/conceptnet5/blob/master/DATA-CREDITS.md), [FreeDict](https://freedict.org/documentation/), [WikDict](https://www.wikdict.com/page/download), [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads), [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights).
