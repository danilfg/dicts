# Legal review checklist for each published release

- [ ] Record canonical upstream URL, exact version or timestamp and the SHA-256 of input archive.
- [ ] Check embedded LICENSE/ATTRIBUTION (KOReader), source TEI header and required source distribution (FreeDict), CC BY-SA terms (WikDict and Wiktionary), or Tatoeba sentence-level attribution.
- [ ] Do not treat the MIT build scripts' license as the license for the dataset.
- [ ] For GPL/GFDL datasets, check corresponding-source and derivative distribution obligations; review license compatibility for combined indexes.
- [ ] Preserve creator names, copyright notices, source/author URLs and license URIs.
- [ ] Mark modifications such as repackaging, filtering and indexing.
- [ ] Keep Tatoeba sentence ID, author and link, and license status; never reuse unrelated audio without permission.
- [ ] Do not imply endorsement by the original projects.
- [ ] Require manifest.json and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md in every release.
- [ ] Reject or quarantine unverified sources rather than assuming public availability permits redistribution.
- [ ] Review any commercial use or combination of incompatible copyleft datasets with qualified counsel before sale.
