Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026. LingoInView is developed by danilfg.

The extension reads YouTube subtitles and video context to translate selected words and create flashcards. Words, translations, context, video titles and identifiers, timestamps, study history, settings and dictionaries are stored locally in Chrome extension storage and IndexedDB.

Dictionary catalogs and packages are downloaded from GitHub and GHCR, which receive normal connection metadata such as IP address and requested resources. Optional Chrome built-in translation may download language models under Google's policies. Selected words and subtitles are not sent to a developer-operated translation or analytics server. External links open third-party sites at your request.

The current version has no account, developer telemetry, advertising tracking or cloud synchronization. The developer does not sell or share extension user data with advertisers. Exports are created at your request; you control their destination.

Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the extension. JSON backups and other exports may contain your study history and video context; keep them secure.

This website has no custom analytics, tracking cookies or registration forms. GitHub Pages may process ordinary hosting logs. Third-party services have their own policies.

Questions and updates: contact the developer via the GitHub profile below. The policy will be updated before new data collection features are introduced.
